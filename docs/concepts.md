# FORME and APEX: direction contract

These are fictional NEXUS concepts. The user supplied the full visual and interaction direction. Implementation follows that pinned brief, code-first for the procedural scene and frame player. No commercial claims, testimonials, affiliations, or messages sent.

## FORME

Mode: Experience, followed by an operable residence explorer. First viewport: graphite architectural drawing sheet; large, left-aligned Host Grotesk statement; exploded seven-storey building occupying the right and extending through the viewport. Fine rules and orange dimension marks carry real geometric meaning. Four scroll beats assemble the building, reveal materials, move the sun and lift the penthouse plan. Mobile and reduced-motion use a saved render of the same scene and stacked copy. Scoped CSS preserves the NEXUS visual system outside this route.

Palette: #121417, #8E9192, #9FB7C4, #F2F3F1, #FF5B1F. Body 17px, minimum 13px. Host Grotesk and Geist Mono.

## APEX

Mode: Experience, followed by service comparison and a local booking demo. First viewport: a single unbadged car in a plum-black studio, with restrained lighting and wide Michroma lettering. Four scroll beats illuminate the car, inspect correction, demonstrate beads and reveal the consultation action. The interim sequence derives from an AI-generated still and must be labelled accordingly. The final asset is a continuous orbit video supplied by the user.

Palette: #0E0C11, #1C1A21, #ECE8EE, #E5263F. Body 17px, minimum 13px. Michroma, Inter Tight and Geist Mono.
