# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and managers of small service businesses (clinics, hotels, salons, trades, agencies) who lose time and leads to slow follow-up and an unremarkable website. They are evaluating whether a small studio can be trusted with their website and their customer workflow. Market and language are an open decision: English and region-neutral until decided.

## Product Purpose

NEXUS is a two-discipline studio selling (1) custom web design and (2) workflow automation (lead capture, qualification, scheduling, CRM). The site exists to win real clients: success is a visitor understanding the offer and getting in touch.

## Positioning

One partner for both the website a customer sees and the system that handles the inquiry behind it. The site proves this with working, honest demos (scripted assistant, workflow simulator, editable ROI calculator) instead of claims.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React 19 + Vite + TypeScript, React Router, ten routes. Static hosting.
- Interactive demos are scripted; no live AI, no messages sent, no bookings made.
- Inquiry delivery needs `VITE_CONTACT_ENDPOINT`; booking needs `VITE_BOOKING_URL`; email needs `VITE_CONTACT_EMAIL`. None are configured yet (open).
- Business identity, location and legal details are not yet provided (open).

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real client projects, no people, no prices, no testimonials. The six portfolio pieces (AURA, FORME, LUMEN, APEX, FIELD, STILL) are original concepts and must stay labelled as such. Never fabricate clients, results, logos, testimonials, team members, prices or trademarks.

## Product Principles

1. Show, don't claim: credibility comes from demos a visitor can touch.
2. Honest, but not apologetic: label concepts and simulations once, plainly, not on every element.
3. Readable first: plain language about outcomes a business owner cares about, not slogans.
4. Getting in touch must always work.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text never below 16px; no UI text below 12px. Reduced motion honoured.
